What’s New

What was added and fixed in each version.

NEW IN v3.0.3

v3.0.3 — One extension instead of many.

Your existing bookmarks become your workspace. Everything that naturally grows out of tab and bookmark management, in one place.

Turn your bookmark folders into groups

Just pick the folders you use. Nothing is deleted, and you can move them back anytime.

Live pen (free)

Draw on the page to point things out in online meetings. It fades away after a moment (Alt+Shift+D).

Slim favicon rail (free)

A thin column of tab icons at the edge of the page, so big screens stay wide.

Control playing tabs from the panel

Volume, playback position and pause. Move videos into a mini window and keep working.

Badges that guide you

A small celebration the first time you use a feature, and a “Next:” hint for what to try.

Now in 15 languages

Seven more languages, including Italian, Dutch, Polish, Turkish, Russian, Simplified Chinese and Hindi.

See what’s new in 3.0.3 →

NEW IN v3.0.1

v3.0.1 — Open, close, and never lose your place.

Tabs are “now”, bookmarks are “later”. Open a project with ▶ and put it away with ■.

▶ opens a Chrome tab group

Tabs opened with ▶ are gathered into a Chrome tab group with the same name and color as your group. ■ closes that tab group.

Switch views from the side column

Tabs, Bookmarks, History, Notes and Steps sit in a column at the edge of the panel, so the list gets the full height.

“+ New Group” and “Add current tab”

Create a group at the top of the list, and save the tab you are on with the button at the bottom. Just pick a group.

“Add to pin bar” from the right-click menu

Right-click a tab or bookmark to keep the sites you use every day on the pin bar.

Deleted by mistake? Undo it

Deleting a group or a saved tab moves it to the trash, with an 8-second “Undo”.

A short animated guide for new users

A five-minute quick start opens when you install (English, Japanese, Korean and Chinese).

Read the quick start →

NEW IN v2.13.0

v2.13.0 — Arrange it your way.

Your tab order and your mode switcher, just where you want them.

Reorder tabs by dragging

Drag open tabs in the side panel to reorder them. The browser’s tab strip follows.

Mode switcher at the edge

Put it in a column on the left edge if the panel is on the left, or the right edge if it is on the right (choose it in Settings). It can collapse into a slim icon column.

See your shortcut

The shortcut (such as Alt+T) now shows next to the logo. Click it to open Chrome’s shortcut settings if another app is in the way.

The window stays when you close the last tab

Closing the last tab opens a new one first, so the Chrome window doesn’t vanish.

Easier bookmark cleanup

The “Delete all duplicates” bar stays on screen while you scroll. A Stop button is available while it runs, and closing it mid-run no longer gets it stuck.

NEW IN v2.12.0

v2.12.0 — Easy to save, find and open.

Get a page into a group the moment you think of it — and a heads-up before your tabs pile up.

Drop to save

Drag the address-bar icon or any link onto a group. Folder-view folders accept drops too.

Paste URLs to add

Ctrl+V adds one or many URLs (several lines are fine). The group with the same site is picked for you.

Recently used groups first

They lead the list wherever you choose a group — paste, “To group”, right-click. Undo is right there after saving.

Sort “Sort later” in bulk

Select saved tabs and move them to another group at once. The bookmarks move with them.

A notice when many tabs are open

Advice at 15+ tabs, a warning at 100+, with “Group them” and “Close the others” (adjustable in Settings).

Copied-URL notice (optional)

Turn it on in Settings and you’ll be told when a URL is copied, so you can paste it into a group.

NEW IN v2.11.0

v2.11.0 — See your work’s progress, too.

Step Tasks + folder view + bulk actions in the Tabs view + thumbnails + a welcome screen built around how you work.

Step Tasks

Split work into steps — a task counts only when every step is done. See today’s and this week’s finished tasks, and link them to a project (group). PRO (available during the trial).

34 work-process templates

Proposals, meeting minutes, monthly reports and more. Choose the number of steps, time and date. Save your own templates, or add a task to Google Calendar.

Folder view

Browse your saved groups like a file explorer. Drag to move, use the mouse back/forward buttons, and middle-click a folder to open all its tabs at once.

Group tabs in bulk from the Tabs view

Every open tab, just like the browser’s tab strip. Select several and add them to a new or existing group. Saved tabs appear dimmed.

Thumbnail view (optional)

Only when you turn it on, a small picture of each tab you visit is kept on your computer. Mail, banking and login pages are never captured, and nothing is sent out.

A welcome screen built around how you work

Answer “How do you want to use it?” and your first groups and notes are set up for you.

v2.9 – v2.10 — Notes, and layouts that fit how you work.

New notes and layout features already in the store (free to use).

Notes & checklists

Free-form notes and checklists in their own full-height view. Colors and pinning included.

Note search & backup
