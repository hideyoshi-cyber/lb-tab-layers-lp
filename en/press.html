Long (about 180 words)

LB TAB Layers is a Chrome extension that organizes your open tabs and bookmarks into project groups — “shelves” — in the side panel. Your tabs are listed by name, so you can tell them apart at a glance and put the finished ones away by project. Press ▶ to open all the pages in a group and ■ to close them. Everything is saved as Chrome bookmarks, so there is no account to create. You can start from a copy of your current bookmarks, and the originals stay exactly where they are. It also includes the tools you need for work: annotations on videos, web pages and images that you can hand over as a PDF or PowerPoint file, screenshots, and notes. It is free to use (up to 5 groups), and every feature is unlocked for the first 30 days. If you buy by December 31, 2026, PRO is paid once with a lifetime license (from January 1, 2027, it becomes an auto-renewing subscription). Students can use PRO free for 3 years by applying. The interface is available in 19 languages. It was built by Liquid Block Inc., a video production company in Kobe, Japan, to spend less time on busywork and more time thinking.

Main features

  • Project groups: sort tabs and bookmarks into groups, open them all with ▶ and close them all with ■ (Bookmarks & groups help)
  • Start from your current bookmarks: line up a copy of the bookmarks you already have; the originals are not moved (Start & keep going help)
  • Tidy up tabs: see open tabs by name and put the finished ones into a group (Tidy up tabs help)
  • Revision notes: mark up videos, web pages and images, and hand them over as a PDF or PowerPoint file (Revision notes help)
  • Screenshots: capture a full page, or every page in a group at once (Screenshots & images help)
  • Notes and Step Tasks: notes linked to pages, and tasks broken into steps (Notes help · Step Tasks help)

Why we built it

At Liquid Block, we manage every project in a single folder: planning, pre-production materials and references, AI research, storyboards, CG, cloud previews, delivery, and the invoice at the end of the month. In pre-production, dozens of reference tabs open at once, and the tab strip alone makes them impossible to tell apart.

In a side panel you can read the names, turn the tabs you collected into a folder, open them all to check, and close them all at once.

We built it to cut that daily friction — less time on busywork, more time to think.
— Liquid Block Inc. (from About Us)
