About your subscription

  • It renews automatically for the same term (3 years, 1 year or 1 month) until you cancel. On each renewal date, your registered card is charged.
  • Your next renewal date is in the email from Stripe. You can also see it by clicking the PRO badge at the top of LB TAB Layers.
  • You can cancel anytime. Use the management page linked in the email from Stripe, or click the PRO badge and choose "Manage or cancel subscription". If you can't find either, email info@liquid-block.com from the address you paid with and write "cancel". We'll do it for you.
  • After you cancel, you keep PRO until the end of the term you paid for. If you cancel by the day before the renewal date, you are not charged for the next term (Terms of Service, Article 4).
  • Your receipt arrives by email from Stripe. For how to use it, see the Quick start and Help.

If you paid with PayPay

  • If you need a receipt, email info@liquid-block.com from the address you paid with. For how to use it, see the Quick start and Help.

For students
