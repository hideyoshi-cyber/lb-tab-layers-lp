LB TAB Layers is a Chrome extension made by a video production studio in Kobe, Japan, to spend less time on busywork and more time thinking. Research, learn, make — we want you to have a tool that takes care of the tedious part before the thinking, while you are still learning.

Students get PRO, with every feature unlocked, free for 3 years.

Who can apply

  • Students enrolled in a school (university, graduate school, college, vocational school, high school, etc.)
  • Any country or region
  • You must be 13 or older. If you are under 18, please get your parent's or guardian's consent before applying

How to apply

  1. Email info@liquid-block.com (subject: "Student application") with:
    • Your name
    • Your school and department
    • One proof of enrollment: a photo of your student ID (you may hide the ID number and photo — the school name, your name and the expiry date are enough), a note from your teacher, or send the email from your school address
  2. After checking, we email you a personal code.
  3. In LB TAB Layers, click "$12.99 PRO" (or "Get PRO"), then enter the code under "Add promotion code" on the payment page. The total becomes $0 and no card is needed (even at $0, you enter an email address for the receipt, and your name and address so that tax can be calculated).

FAQ

What happens after 3 years?

To keep PRO, you can continue with a subscription at the price at that time. The free features stay free.

What if I graduate?

You keep PRO for 3 years from the day you used the code, even after you graduate.

What happens to my student ID photo?

We use it only to confirm that you are a student, and delete it right after checking (Privacy Policy).

For teachers

If you want to use it in a class, apply on behalf of your students and we will send codes for everyone. Teachers can use it too.

Do I need an account?

No. Groups and tabs are saved in your Chrome bookmarks and on your computer, and are not sent to our servers.
