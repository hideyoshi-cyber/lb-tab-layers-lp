Panel layout: resize and collapse the side panel areas

Sep 24, 2026 · By Hideyoshi Saimoto, CEO of Liquid Block Inc. · 3 min read

PostShareShare

The side panel is split into two areas: the group list on top and Solo Tabs / Today’s History below. Drag the handle between them to change their size, or press the chevron button on the lower tab bar to collapse the lower area and give the group list all the room.

How the side panel is laid out

WhereWhat
TopMode bar, search and pin bar
MiddleYour group list
BottomA tab bar that switches between Solo Tabs and Today’s History
Very bottomArchive (only when you have archived groups)

Resize the two areas

Drag the resize handle between the group list and the lower area up or down. The ratio you choose is remembered, so the panel opens the same way next time.

Collapse the lower area

Press the chevron button on the lower tab bar. Solo Tabs and Today’s History fold away, leaving only the tab bar. Press it again to bring them back. Handy when you want the group list to fill the panel.

Suggested setups

  • Tidying up — open the lower area wide and drag Solo Tabs into groups.
  • Working — collapse the lower area and look at your groups only.
  • Looking for something — switch to Today’s History to find a page you just closed.

Practical examples

Video production: a long list of project groups

If you run several jobs at once (one group per client, plus subgroups for edit, audio and delivery), the group list gets long. Drag the handle down so the list fills most of the panel, and collapse the lower area completely while you are editing. You only bring Solo Tabs back when you have a batch of loose tabs to file away.

Research: sorting a pile of tabs

After a long search session you may have thirty tabs open that belong to no group. Drag the handle up so Solo Tabs gets the room, then drag each tab into the right group. When the pile is gone, press the chevron to collapse the lower area again.

Freelancing: finding the page you just closed

You closed a client’s invoice page a minute ago and need it back. Open the lower area, switch to the history tab and look for it. The split you set earlier is still there, so you don’t have to resize anything first.

Students: a small laptop screen

On a small screen the side panel is already short. Collapsing the lower area during a lecture leaves your course groups visible in full, and one click brings Solo Tabs back when you open too many tabs while reading.

Tips and limits

  • The handle works vertically. It changes the height of the two areas. It does not change the width of the side panel itself; that is controlled by Chrome.
  • There are minimum sizes. The group list can take at most about 90% of the space and at least about 15%, so neither area can be dragged away completely. To hide the lower area entirely, use the chevron button.
  • The ratio is remembered on this browser. It is stored locally, so another computer starts with its own default layout.
  • Collapsing does not close anything. Your Solo Tabs and history are still there when you expand the area again.
  • History is a PRO feature. In the free version, trying to open it shows an upgrade message. Solo Tabs and the group list work in both versions.
  • Group limits still apply. A taller group list doesn’t change the free limit of 5 groups; it just makes the groups easier to see.

Frequently asked questions: more about the layout

Frequently asked questions

Q. How do I make the group list bigger?

Drag the handle between the group list and the lower area downwards, or press the chevron button on the lower tab bar to collapse the lower area.

Q. Is the size I choose remembered?

Yes. The ratio is saved and used the next time you open the side panel.

Q. Can I change the width of the side panel?

Not from within LB TAB Layers. The width is controlled by Chrome. The extension only controls how the height is shared between the group list and the lower area.

Q. Does collapsing the lower area close my Solo Tabs?

No. It only hides the list. The tabs stay open in the browser, and the list reappears when you press the chevron button again.

Q. Is Today's History available in the free version?

No. Browsing history in the side panel is a PRO feature. The free version shows an upgrade message when you try to open it. PRO features are also available during the free trial.
