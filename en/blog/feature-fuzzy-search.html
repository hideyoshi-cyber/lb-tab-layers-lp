Search that forgives typos: finding saved tabs fast

Sep 24, 2026 · Liquid Block Inc. · 3 min read

PostShareShare

The LB TAB Layers search box looks through the titles, URLs and notes of your saved tabs, Solo Tabs and Today’s History. It forgives a one-letter typo, understands abbreviations such as “ytb” for YouTube, and puts the best matches first.

What it searches

Type in the search box and your saved tabs, Solo Tabs and Today’s History are filtered at the same time. Results are split into matching groups (folders) and matching sites.

How it matches

You typeIt finds
google“Google” first, then “Google Drive”, then pages that only mention Google in the URL
gogleGoogle — one wrong letter is fine for words of 4 letters or more
ytbYouTube — letters in order, close together
drive googlePages that contain both words, in any order
invoiceA tab whose note mentions “invoice”, even if the title doesn’t

Exact matches beat matches at the start of a title, which beat matches at the start of a word, which beat matches in the middle. Upper and lower case, and full-width and half-width characters, are treated the same.

Google suggestions

While you type, Google search suggestions appear too. If a page isn’t among your saved tabs, you can jump straight to a web search.

Search by meaning with AI search

When the words you remember aren’t in the title — “the slides for next week’s meeting” — use AI search (PRO, and during the free trial).

Frequently asked questions

Q. Does search work if I make a typo?

Yes. For words of four letters or more, one wrong, missing, extra or swapped letter is fine — “gogle” finds Google.

Q. What does the search box look through?

The titles, URLs and notes of your saved tabs, plus Solo Tabs and Today’s History.
