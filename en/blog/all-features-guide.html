LB TAB Layers feature guide: every feature, mode by mode

Sep 24, 2026 · Liquid Block Inc. · 6 min read

PostShareShare

LB TAB Layers is a Chrome side-panel extension for managing tabs. A mode bar at the top switches between five modes — Tabs, Clean, Shots, AI and DB (dashboard). At its core is the Tabs mode: you gather tabs into groups, and every group is saved as a real Chrome bookmark folder, so your data stays even if you remove the extension.

In the tables below, Free means the feature works in the free version. PRO means it is part of the PRO version — and every PRO feature is unlocked during the 3-month free trial.

Frequently asked questions

Q. What can LB TAB Layers do?

Its core is tab management: you gather tabs into groups that are saved as Chrome bookmark folders. On top of that it cleans up duplicate and dead bookmarks, takes bulk screenshots, reads RSS feeds, offers a focus timer, an AI sidebar and a full-screen dashboard. A mode bar switches between the Tabs, Clean, Shots, AI and DB modes.

Q. Which features are free?

Most of them: saving tabs, bookmark sync, search, opening and closing tabs in bulk, Clean mode, bulk screenshots, RSS and the focus timer all work in the free version. The dashboard, AI search, Today’s History and the overlay display are PRO features, and all of them are unlocked during the 3-month free trial.
