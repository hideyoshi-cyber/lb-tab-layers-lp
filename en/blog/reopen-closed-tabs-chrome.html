How to reopen closed tabs in Chrome (and stop needing to)

Oct 8, 2026 · Liquid Block Inc. · 4 min read

PostShareShare

Press Ctrl+Shift+T (⌘+Shift+T on Mac) to reopen the last tab you closed in Chrome; press it again to go further back. For older tabs, open History → Recently closed or search your full history with Ctrl+H. To never lose an important tab, save it to a group before you close it.

1. Ctrl+Shift+T: the last closed tab

Press Ctrl+Shift+T (⌘+Shift+T on Mac). Chrome reopens the tab you closed most recently, with its back history. Press it again to keep going back. If the last thing you closed was a whole window, it reopens the window with all its tabs.

You can do the same with the mouse: right-click an empty spot on the tab bar and choose Reopen closed tab.

2. Recently closed: pick the one you want

Open the ⋮ menu → History. The Recently closed list shows tabs and windows you closed lately; click one to bring it back. Useful when the tab you want isn’t the last one.

3. History: anything from the past days

Press Ctrl+H (⌘+Y on Mac) and search by a word from the page title or the site name. This works even for tabs you closed days ago — as long as you remember something about them.

4. After a crash or restart

  • If Chrome closed unexpectedly, it usually offers to Restore your pages when you open it again.
  • To always come back to where you were, go to Settings → On startup and choose Continue where you left off.

Where these methods run out

MethodGood forLimit
Ctrl+Shift+TThe tab you just closedGoes back in order only
Recently closedA recent tab or windowA short list
HistoryOlder pagesYou must remember what to search for; history can be cleared
Continue where you left offRestartsBrings back every tab, not just the ones you need

All of them are ways to recover from losing a tab. The better habit is not losing it in the first place.

Stop needing to reopen tabs

In LB TAB Layers, tabs you want to keep go into a group in the side panel, and each one is saved as a Chrome bookmark. Then closing is safe:

  • ■ on a group closes its tabs; ▶ opens them all again — tomorrow, next week or next month.
  • Open tabs that aren’t saved yet are listed under Solo Tabs, so you can see what would be lost before you close the window.
  • Because groups are bookmarks, clearing your history doesn’t touch them.

Frequently asked questions

Q. What is the shortcut to reopen a closed tab in Chrome?

Ctrl+Shift+T on Windows, ⌘+Shift+T on Mac. Press it repeatedly to reopen tabs in the order you closed them.

Q. How do I reopen a tab I closed days ago?

Search your history with Ctrl+H (⌘+Y on Mac) using a word from the page title or the site name. If your history was cleared, only a bookmark or a saved group will bring it back.

Q. How do I make Chrome reopen my tabs after a restart?

Go to Settings → On startup and choose Continue where you left off.
