Draw on a web page while screen sharing
— a live pen for Chrome

Nov 22, 2026 · By Hideyoshi Saimoto, CEO of Liquid Block Inc. · 4 min read

PostShareShare

Press Alt+Shift+D to bring up the LB TAB Layers live pen, then drag on the page to draw circles and lines. The lines fade after a moment, so you can use it like a laser pointer while sharing your screen. Press Esc to finish. Nothing you draw is saved or sent, and the live pen is free.

You are sharing your screen in a call and saying “top right, just under that, the blue button…” while everyone else follows your cursor. Drawing a circle on the page says it in one go.

Three ways to start drawing

  • Press Alt+Shift+D.
  • Right-click the page and choose Draw with the live pen.
  • Click the pen button at the top of the side panel.

A small toolbar appears on the screen. Then just drag on the page to draw.

What the toolbar does

ToolWhat it does
FadingLines disappear after a moment. For pointing things out
StaysLines stay until you clear them. For building up an explanation
Use pagePauses the pen so you can use the page underneath, for example to press a video’s play button
ColorChanges the line color
Clear allRemoves what you drew. The C key does the same
ClosePuts the pen away. Esc does the same

If the toolbar sits where you want to draw, drag it somewhere else.

When it helps

  • Explaining on a call: circle the number you want people to look at.
  • Design reviews: draw a line where the spacing should be wider.
  • Walking someone through a task: draw an arrow to the button they should press next.

If you need to keep what you marked and hand it to someone, LB TAB Layers revision notes are the better fit: they capture the screen and let you add numbered pins and instructions you can export.

Why the lines fade

When you are only pointing at something, lines that stay get in the way. Every time the conversation moves on, you would have to stop and erase them. Fading lines clear themselves as you talk, so you can draw without thinking about cleaning up. Switch to Stays only when you want the drawing to build up.

Good to know

  • Pages you can’t draw on: Chrome’s settings pages, the Chrome Web Store and similar pages.
  • Nothing is kept: what you draw is not saved or sent anywhere. When you close the pen, nothing remains.
  • Changing the key: you can change Alt+Shift+D in Chrome’s extension shortcut settings. See shortcut keys for how.
  • Price: the live pen is a free feature.

There is much more in the side panel than a pen. The operation guide walks through the main actions, and the quick start gets you set up in a few minutes.

Frequently asked questions

Q. Can I draw on a web page while sharing my screen?

Yes. Press Alt+Shift+D to bring up the LB TAB Layers live pen and drag to draw circles and lines on the page. The lines fade after a moment.

Q. Can I keep the lines on the page?

Switch to Stays and the lines remain until you press Clear all. Closing the pen removes them.

Q. Can I click buttons on the page while the pen is open?

Click Use page. The pen pauses and you can use the page underneath. Choose a pen again to keep drawing.

Q. Is the live pen free, and is anything saved?

The live pen is free. Nothing you draw is saved or sent anywhere.
