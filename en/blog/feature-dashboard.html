The dashboard: organize groups and bookmarks on a big screen

Sep 24, 2026 · Liquid Block Inc. · 4 min read

PostShareShare

The dashboard is where you organize large numbers of groups and bookmarks that feel cramped in the side panel — on a full browser tab. Open it from DB in the mode bar. It is a PRO feature, available during the 3-month free trial.

What you can do in the dashboard

FeatureWhat it does
Card listSee the tabs of a group as cards. Turn on the thumbnail setting to see a page preview when you hover over a card
Import bookmarksBrowse your existing Chrome bookmark folders and bring them into your groups
SortingDefault order, highest stars first, or lowest stars first
Drag to moveDrag tabs between groups — you can even create a new destination folder on the way
Notes, stars and tagsWrite a note on a tab, rate it and tag it
Cleanup toolsMerge duplicate folders, remove duplicate bookmarks in one go, and check for dead links
Archive managementRestore or delete archived groups in bulk

How to open it

Click DB in the mode bar at the top of the side panel. The dashboard opens in a new tab.

Bring in your existing bookmarks

The left side of the dashboard lists your Chrome bookmark folders. Everything you collected in the bookmarks bar or “Other bookmarks” before installing LB TAB Layers can be imported into your groups while you browse it. It is the ideal place to reorganize old bookmarks.

Side panel or dashboard?

  • Side panel — while you work: open, save and close tabs.
  • Dashboard — once a week, when you sit down to sort, clean up and tag.

Frequently asked questions

Q. Where do I open the dashboard?

Click DB in the mode bar at the top of the side panel; the dashboard opens in a new tab. It is a PRO feature and is available during the 3-month free trial.

Q. Can I move my existing bookmarks into LB TAB Layers?

Yes. The left side of the dashboard shows your Chrome bookmark folders, and you can import them into LB TAB Layers groups from there.
