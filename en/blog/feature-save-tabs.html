Three ways to save tabs: add button, drag and drop, right-click

Sep 24, 2026 · By Hideyoshi Saimoto, CEO of Liquid Block Inc. · 4 min read

PostShareShare

There are three ways to save a tab in LB TAB Layers: 1) click Add current tab on a group to save the page you are on, 2) drag an open tab from the list into a group, or 3) right-click a tab and choose Save to Folder. The moment you save, the tab is also saved as a Chrome bookmark.

1. The Add current tab button

The quickest way to keep the page you are reading. Click Add current tab on the group you want, and the tab goes straight into it.

2. Drag and drop

Solo Tabs at the bottom of the side panel lists the open tabs that are not in any group. Drag one of them onto a group. It is the handy way to sort many tabs in one sitting.

3. Right-click → Save to Folder

Right-click a tab in Solo Tabs, choose Save to Folder, then pick the destination. Useful when you have so many groups that dragging gets awkward.

What happens when you save

  • If the same URL is already in that group, you’ll see Already added and it won’t be saved twice. Small differences such as a trailing slash are treated as the same URL.
  • If the same URL is in a different group, you’ll be asked to confirm first.
  • A thumbnail of the page is captured as you save (when thumbnails are turned on in the settings).

Working with saved tabs

To…Do this
Open itClick the saved tab
Change its display nameRight-click → Rename (the original page title is kept)
Move it to another groupDrag it, or right-click → Move to Group
Add a noteRight-click → the item with the notepad icon
Remove it from the groupRight-click → Delete

Saved tabs stay saved when you close them. The side panel shows at a glance which saved tabs are currently open and which are closed.

Practical examples

Video production: collect references while browsing

You are looking through footage libraries and motion references for a new project. Each time you find a good page, click Add current tab on the project group. The page is saved and stays open, so you keep browsing without losing your place.

Research: sort a messy session at the end of the day

After a long session you may have thirty open tabs that belong to no group. They all appear in Solo Tabs. Drag each useful one onto the right group and close the rest. Dragging is the fastest way when you are sorting many tabs at once.

Freelancing: many clients, many groups

When you have too many groups to drag onto comfortably, right-click a tab in Solo Tabs, choose Save to Folder and pick the destination. It works the same however far down the list the group is.

Students: keep sources next to your notes

Save each source for an essay into one group, then right-click the saved tab and use the notepad item to write why you kept it. Saved tabs stay saved when you close them, so you can clear your tab bar and still return to every source later.

Tips and limits

  • Duplicates are checked per group. The same URL in the same group shows Already added and is skipped. The same URL in another group asks you to confirm first.
  • Closing a saved tab does not delete it. It stays in the group and is shown as closed. To remove it, right-click it and choose Delete.
  • Thumbnails are optional. A page thumbnail is captured when you save only if thumbnails are turned on in the settings.
  • Every saved tab is a Chrome bookmark. Your data stays in Chrome, and deleting a saved tab here also removes that bookmark.
  • Free and PRO. The free version limits the number of groups, so there are fewer places to save into. PRO and the free trial remove that limit.

Frequently asked questions

Q. What happens if I save the same page twice?

If the same URL is already in that group you will see “Already added” and it is not saved again. If it is in another group, you will be asked to confirm.

Q. Can I rename a saved tab?

Yes. Right-click the saved tab and choose Rename to change how it appears in the side panel. The original page title is kept.

Q. Can I save a tab to a group that is not at the top of the list?

Yes. Right-click the tab in Solo Tabs, choose Save to Folder and pick any group, or drag the tab onto it.

Q. How do I remove a saved tab I no longer need?

Right-click the saved tab and choose Delete. This also removes the matching Chrome bookmark.
