Why we built it

We manage every project in a single folder: planning, pre-production materials and references, AI research, storyboards, CG, cloud previews, delivery, and the invoice at the end of the month. That is exactly why we keep it as a bookmark folder.

In pre-production, dozens of reference tabs open at once, and the tab strip alone makes them impossible to tell apart. In a side panel you can read the names, turn the tabs you collected into a folder, open them all to check, and close them all at once. We built it to cut that daily friction — less time on busywork, more time to think.

What we care about

  • Separate collecting from sorting: sorting while you search wears your head out. Collect first, then sort in one go.
  • Purpose, structure, then work: decide the goal, break it into steps you can finish, then just do the steps.
  • Your data stays with you: groups and tabs live in your Chrome bookmarks, notes stay on this computer, and nothing is sent to our servers. See our privacy policy.
