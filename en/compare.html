vs. Chrome built-in

What it does better than Chrome's built-in tools.

It saves to the same Chrome bookmarks you already use — the built-in tools keep working, LB TAB Layers just fills the gaps.

Tab groups

LB TAB LayersSubgroups, Archive for finished projects, presets that build a structure in one click, and notes and stars on each tab
Bookmarks
Chrome built-inYou can "Open all" in a folder, but not close them all or see which are open
LB TAB LayersOpen everything with ▶, close everything with ■, and see open and saved tabs in one list
Bookmark cleanup
Chrome built-inNo way to find duplicates or dead links
LB TAB LayersFinds duplicates across all bookmarks and checks dead links, then cleans them up in bulk
Search
Chrome built-inOnly the exact text you type. Tabs and bookmarks are searched separately
LB TAB LayersSearches saved tabs, open tabs and today's history (PRO) together, with fuzzy matching
Saving and tidying
Chrome built-inSave one page at a time with the star; unsaved tabs pile up
