FAQ

Is it free to use?

Yes. Saving, searching, and opening or closing everything at once are free. For the first 3 months, you can try every feature. PRO is a one-time purchase, with no monthly fee.

What happens to my current bookmarks?

You keep using them as they are. Bring your Chrome bookmarks in as-is and organize them.

What happens to my data if I remove it?

Saved tabs remain as Chrome bookmarks. Settings such as themes and colors are deleted.

Which browsers does it work with?

It works with Google Chrome (114 or later), on Windows, Mac, Linux, and ChromeOS. The Edge version is in preparation.
