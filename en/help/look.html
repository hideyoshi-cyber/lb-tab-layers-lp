Theme PRO

Choose from 40 color schemes (3 on the free plan)

Where: Settings › Look › Theme

Choose from 40 color schemes in Settings › Look › Theme (3 on the free plan). The scheme you choose also applies to the Dashboard and other screens. With PRO, you can also make your own scheme from three colors: background, text and accent.

Original theme PRO

Make your own color scheme from three colors: background, text and accent

Where: First item in the theme list

From the first item in the theme list, pick three colors (background, text and accent) to make your own color scheme. PRO feature (available during the free trial).

Text & rows

Text contrast, size, row height, weight and tone

Where: Settings › Look › Text & rows

In Settings › Look › Text & rows, change the text contrast, size, row height, weight and tone. Set it to a size that is easy on your eyes.

Search settings

Find a setting by typing a word

Where: Top of Settings

Type a word in the box at the top of Settings to find a setting. Handy for settings you can't locate.

Behavior settings

Turn on or off: Chrome tab groups when opening together, notices, and helpful extras

Where: Settings › Behavior

In Settings › Behavior, turn on or off whether groups open as Chrome tab groups, whether notices appear, and whether helpful extras are used.
