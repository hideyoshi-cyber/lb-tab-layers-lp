Live pen

Draw lines right on the page. They fade after about 2 seconds. There is also a pen that stays. Nothing is saved or sent

Where: Bottom of the right rail / Alt+Shift+D

With the live pen you can draw on top of any page. Nothing is saved or sent. Switch between “Fading” and “Stays”, pick from 6 colors, and turn on “Use page” to click the page underneath. Press C to clear everything and Esc to close.

Copy as Markdown

Copy a group or tab as text with links

Where: A group's “…” / the buttons on a row

From a group's “…” or the buttons on a row, copy a group or a page as text with links (Markdown). Paste it straight into notes or chat.

Copy & export in bulk PRO

Copy or export the selected items as URLs, titles and URLs, Markdown, HTML or CSV

Where: “Copy” in select mode

In select mode, use “Copy” to copy or export the selected items as URLs only, titles and URLs, Markdown, HTML or CSV. PRO feature (available during the free trial).

Hand to AI PRO

Copy pages and notes together, in a form you can paste into an AI

Where: Right-click a group / select mode

From a group's right-click menu, or in select mode, copy pages and notes together in a form that is easy to paste into an AI. PRO feature (available during the free trial).

Send to NotebookLM PRO

Send the selected pages to a notebook

Where: Right-click / select mode

Send the selected pages to a NotebookLM notebook from the right-click menu or select mode. You need to be signed in to Google. PRO feature (available during the free trial).

Send to social media PRO

Send a note to the post screen of X, Threads, Bluesky and others

Where: The bookmark's note screen

Send a bookmark's note straight to the post screen of X, Threads, Bluesky and other services. You decide there whether to post. You need to be signed in to each service. PRO feature (available during the free trial).

Google Sheets sync

Save groups and bookmarks to a sheet, and restore them from a sheet

Where: Settings › Data

In Settings › Data, save your groups and bookmarks to Google Sheets, or restore them from a sheet. You need to be signed in to Google.
